# Pikes Peak State College Cyber Club
The Pikes Peak State College Cyber Club is a group of computer science and cybersecurity students (with a few others thrown in) that meets bi-weekly during the Fall and Spring semesters. During our meetings we discuss cybersecurity tools such as Kali Linux, John the Ripper, Hashcat, NetCat, NMAP, and more. 

## Capture The Flag (CTF)
One of our favorite things to do is capture the flag or CTF events. We participate in a variety of CTF events, including National Cyber League (NCL) - both individual and team, HackTheBox, and other CTF competitions.
